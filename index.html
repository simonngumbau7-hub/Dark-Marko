Your Routine in Simple Terms

Skincare shoud not be a 10+ Step Compilication! Just:

Cleanse
➡️
Treat

➡️

Step 1 : Cleanse

Cleansing removes dirt, sweat, excess oil, makeup and sunscreen.
A clean surface allows serums and moisturizers to contact the skin directly for them to work nicely.

Step 2 : Treat

This is where we target dark marks/pigments using active products.
Since we cleansed our skin, we know our serum will spread more evenly and contact the skin directly for it to work effectively.

Step 3 : Moisturize

Why? After treating, we will help the skin hold onto water so it stays comfortable and feels smoother

Step 3 : Protect

This is the most important step after treatment
While our serum is fading dark spots, continued sun exposure can reverse it and make the dark spots visible making you think the serum isnt working at all
Our sunscreen protects progress by preventing dark marks from getting darker
